## Description (limit 4000)

en-US
```
Group Bet is the weekly football pool for your group of friends, without the spreadsheet or the WhatsApp mess. Everyone picks the same official weekly coupon, the app scores it, and the table updates as results come in.

ONE COUPON, EVERY GROUP
- Pick 1, X or 2 for each match, with doubles and triples up to the round's limit.
- Your picks count in every group you belong to: work friends, family, the old team.
- Change your picks any time until the deadline.

SEE HOW YOUR GROUP PLAYED
- After the deadline, see everyone's picks and how the group voted on each match.
- As results come in, every pick is marked right or wrong.
- Compare your coupon with any friend's, side by side.

LEADERBOARDS AND STATS
- Season table, hit rates and best weeks for every member.
- A group pick: the admins fill one shared coupon from the members' votes.

YOUR GROUP, YOUR RULES
- Create a group in seconds and share an invite link or a 6-letter code.
- Open groups let anyone with the code in; closed groups need an admin to approve.
- Several admins per group.

PRIVATE AND FREE
- Sign in with Apple or Google. Only members of your groups see your picks.
- No money is staked, paid or won. No ads, no tracking.
- Delete your account and data any time from your profile.

Group Bet is a free game and isn't affiliated with any betting or lottery operator.
```
