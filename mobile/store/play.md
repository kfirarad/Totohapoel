## App content
- **Privacy policy:** https://groupbet.kfir.app/privacy/
- **App access:** Sign-in is required (Google). There's no password account, so Play reviewers use their own Google account. After sign-in they join the demo group with code in `review-notes.md`.
- **Ads:** No.
- **Content rating (IARC):**
  - Category: "All other app types".
  - Violence, sex, language and drugs: no.
  - Simulated gambling: yes. Users predict match outcomes with no money, currency or prizes.
  - Real-money gambling: no.
  - Users interact or share content: yes (names, photos, group names inside private groups).
  - Shares location: no. Digital purchases: no.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over only, so the families policy doesn't apply.
- **News app:** No.
- **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** none. **Health:** none.
- **Data safety:**
  - Data is collected; nothing is shared with third parties.
  - Encrypted in transit: yes.
  - Users can request deletion: yes. Delete account is in Profile, and https://groupbet.kfir.app/support/ covers it too.
  - Collected, required, for App functionality and Account management:
    - Personal info: Name, Email address, User IDs.
    - Photos and videos: Photos. Optional, profile and group photo.
    - App activity: Other user-generated content (picks, group names).
    - Device or other IDs: push token. Optional, only if notifications are allowed.
  - Not collected: location, contacts, financial, health, messages, files, calendar, web history, diagnostics.
