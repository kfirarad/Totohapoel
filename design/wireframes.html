Low/mid-fi, grayscale + one accent. UI copy in Hebrew RTL (primary audience); screens 2b and 7 are repeated in English LTR to show the mirrored layout. Under each frame: a short rationale and the RN/Expo building blocks it maps to. Built only from logical properties (start/end), so RTL/LTR mirroring is automatic in RN with marginStart/paddingEnd.

Decisions & open questions

  • Settings via Members ⚙ + switcher sheet, not a 4th tab — tab bar stays identical across groups/roles; settings are infrequent.
  • Profile = modal, global across groups. Personal stats live in the group Stats tab.
  • Column switcher segmented (Toto 16 / custom) only when a group has >1 open column in the same week.
  • Compare (8b) is a diff view ("only differences") rather than the web's full-column swap.
  • Decided: "1" always sits under the home team: right in RTL, left in LTR. The picks row mirrors with the teams row; never pinned to LTR.
  • Decided: every group admin can edit the group bet; it locks at the column deadline.
  • Decided: members who join mid-season see the full-season leaderboard (their one bet already counted in past rounds).
